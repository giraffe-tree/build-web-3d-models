# Screenshot review ledger

The two builder rounds below were judged from browser-rendered 1440 × 900 pixels, not from source code or a checklist. A later independent critic received only the original request and rendered evidence, never the implementation or the builder's score. Its lower status is authoritative.

## Round 1 — construction/presentation gate

Evidence reviewed during the isolated run: hero, orbit A, orbit B, neutral material, and subject proof. The repository retains `screenshots/round-1/hero.png` as the compact visual checkpoint.

Three largest visible defects, in order:

1. **Hero crop destroyed the silhouette.** The top leader and upper leaf mass were cut by the frame, so the crown envelope could not be judged.
2. **Foliage read as near-black sparse punctuation.** Small fans and insufficient front fill let the branch scaffold dominate; the result looked like a procedural skeleton rather than a finished canopy.
3. **Ground treatment competed with the root flare.** Large flat moss islands, black leaf litter, and exposed pointed root tips made the contact zone feel diagrammatic.

Highest-impact change: pulled all full-tree cameras back and lowered their targets so the full crown/root relationship fit. The same pass increased deterministic leaf coverage, brightened front/leaf response, reduced and softened moss/litter, and buried root tips below the ground skin.

Visible result: the complete silhouette fits, 4,970 fans form a legible but still open early-autumn canopy, the trunk remains the centre of mass, and the root collar contacts the platform without the earlier starburst.

Round-1 score from pixels: silhouette/proportion 14/25, construction/attachment 13/20, material/light 10/20, surface detail/variation 8/15, motion/interaction evidence 6/10, Web presentation 7/10 = **58/100 (partial)**.

## Round 2 — finish/runtime gate

Evidence reviewed during the isolated run: the complete five-view set. The repository retains `screenshots/round-2/hero.png` as the compact checkpoint and binds the exact final set under `screenshots/final/` by SHA-256 in `quality-evidence.json`.

Three largest visible defects before the final correction, in order:

1. **The close proof was crowded and did not prove the defining fan/notch or veins.** The camera looked through a thicket rather than presenting the feature.
2. **Fine shadow submission exceeded the declared call budget.** The late review sample reported 44 calls and 368,484 frame triangles.
3. **The reverse-lit orbit flattened colour separation.** The neutral material view was retained to distinguish this deliberate back/side-light condition from a broken material.

Highest-impact change: introduced a subject-proof state assembled from the same leaf, vein, bark, short-shoot and petiole modules. It isolates five fans at grazing-neutral light, making the notch, diverging veins and biological attachment inspectable. Low-value tertiary/leaf shadow casters were reduced without changing visible model geometry.

Visible/measured result: the final proof has clear fan silhouettes and vein rhythm; the hero keeps its contact shadow; frame submission dropped to 38 calls and 301,888 triangles while model triangles stayed at 269,792.

Builder's provisional score from pixels: silhouette/proportion 19/25, construction/attachment 16/20, material/light 14/20, surface detail/variation 11/15, motion/interaction 9/10, Web presentation 9/10 = **78/100**.

Remaining finish notes: the mature crown is intentionally open and sculptural rather than botanically dense; the reverse-lit orbits are paler than the hero; and the proof arrangement is diagrammatic by design.

## Independent critic authority

The blind critic scored the exact final asset at **56/100 — partial**, overriding the builder's provisional complete claim. The largest pixel-visible failures were the procedural lattice of repeated straight branches, shelf-like repeated canopy pads, triangular root flare and blocky ground shadow, weak cross-view material separation, and missing temporal proof for the requested light wind. The supposed feature-isolation proof also remained a dense cluster rather than visibly isolating the defining leaf/short-shoot system.

Final repository status: **partial**. See `independent-critic.md` for the full review and `quality-evidence.json` for the bound rubric and screenshot hashes.
